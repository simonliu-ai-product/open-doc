Add a Comments panel to the viewer (`M`, or the Comments button in the header). Review notes left on the document are listed in order with the words of the element each is about, pinned on the page with a number, and marked in the page rail; clicking one goes to it, Resolve takes its marker out of the source, and New comment goes straight to picking an element with the note open. "Copy request for the agent" copies `/apply-comments docs/<id>`, and the agent's edits then show under Changes, which no longer counts the comment markers themselves.
