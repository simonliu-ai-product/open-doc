The viewer remembers where you were. The browser's home page lists the documents you opened most recently (a document deleted or renamed since drops off the list). A document's browser tab shows its title and the page you are on, with a `●` while there are edits not saved yet. Exports to PDF, HTML, Word and images are saved under the document's title instead of its id, with characters no system allows in a file name taken out.
